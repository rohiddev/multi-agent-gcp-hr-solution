# Employee Benefits Overview

## Health Benefits
The company offers medical, dental, and vision coverage for all full-time employees.

## Retirement
Employees may contribute to a 401(k) plan. The company provides a dollar-for-dollar match of up to 4% of eligible compensation.

## Insurance
Basic life insurance is provided at 1x annual salary. Additional voluntary coverage is available.

## Wellness
Employees have access to an employee assistance program (EAP) and annual wellness incentives.

## Effective Date
Plan year begins January 1, 2025.
