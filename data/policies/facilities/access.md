# Building Access and Facilities Guide

## Badge Access
All employees must badge in at building entrances. Do not hold doors open for unknown individuals.

## Reporting Issues
Report building access issues, maintenance problems, or safety concerns to facilities via the internal portal or email facilities@company.com.

## Meeting Rooms
Meeting rooms can be booked through the calendar system. Rooms must be left clean and equipment returned to storage.

## Parking
Employees must display a valid parking permit. Visitor parking is available in the front lot.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
