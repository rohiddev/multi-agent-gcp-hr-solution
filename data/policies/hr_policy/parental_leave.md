# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility
Full-time employees who have completed 90 days of service are eligible for parental leave.

## Leave Duration
Parental leave provides up to 12 weeks of paid leave for eligible parents.

## Types
- Birth parent: up to 12 weeks of paid leave, including recovery time.
- Non-birth parent: up to 12 weeks of paid leave.
- Adoption and foster placement: up to 12 weeks of paid leave.

## Process
Employees must notify HR at least 30 days before the expected leave date when possible.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
