# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Overview
Full-time employees accrue 20 PTO days per year. PTO days are accrued on a bi-weekly basis.

## Carryover
Unused PTO days may be carried over up to a maximum of 40 days. Any days beyond 40 are forfeited at the end of the calendar year.

## Requesting PTO
Employees must request PTO through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance. Manager approval is required.

## Payout
Upon termination, unused PTO up to the annual accrual cap (20 days) is paid out.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
