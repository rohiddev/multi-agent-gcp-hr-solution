# Compliance Training Policy

## Annual Training
All employees are required to complete annual compliance training. The deadline is December 31 of each year.

## Required Courses
- Code of Conduct
- Information Security Awareness
- Anti-Harassment and Discrimination
- Data Privacy and Protection

## New Hires
New employees must complete all required training within 30 days of their start date.

## Tracking
Training completion is tracked in the learning management system (LMS).

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
