# Procurement Procedures

## Request Process
All procurement requests must be submitted through the procurement portal.

## Approval Thresholds
- Under $1,000: manager approval
- $1,000 to $5,000: manager and department head approval
- Over $5,000: manager, department head, and finance approval

## Vendor Selection
New vendors require a security and compliance review before a purchase order can be issued.

## Reimbursement
Expense reimbursements must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date with valid receipts.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
