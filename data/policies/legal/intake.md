# Legal Intake Process

## Submitting a Request
All legal requests must be submitted through the legal intake portal.

## Required Information
Include the contract type, counterparty name, business purpose, and requested completion date.

## Review Timeline
Standard review requests are completed within 5 business days. Urgent requests require a business justification.

## NDAs
Use the standard NDA template for mutual NDAs. Custom NDAs require legal review.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
