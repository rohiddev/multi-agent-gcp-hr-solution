# Purchasing Approval Thresholds

## Overview
All employee purchases must follow the enterprise purchasing policy. Purchases may require
manager, department head, and/or finance approval depending on the estimated cost.

## Approval Path

| Estimated Cost | Approvers |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Manager |
| $1,000 to $4,999 | Manager + Department Head |
| $5,000 and above | Manager + Department Head + Finance |

## Required Information
When creating a purchase requisition, include:
- Item or service description
- Quantity
- Estimated cost
- Business justification
- Preferred vendor (if known)

## Exclusions
This policy does not apply to regulated medical devices, pharmaceuticals, or clinical
supplies. Those purchases must follow the clinical procurement policy.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
