# Payroll FAQ

## How often is payroll processed?
Payroll is processed bi-weekly. Pay dates are every other Friday.

## How do I update my direct deposit?
Direct deposit changes can be made in the payroll portal. Changes must be submitted by the 10th of the month to take effect that month.

## When are W2s available?
W2 forms are available in the payroll portal by January 31 each year.

## Can I change my tax withholdings?
Yes. Submit a new W4 form through the payroll portal.

## Who do I contact for payroll issues?
For complex or sensitive payroll issues, open a payroll case through the HR portal.
