# Provider Credentialing Process

## Overview
Provider credentialing verifies that healthcare providers meet the required qualifications,
licenses, certifications, and experience to participate in the network.

## New Provider Onboarding
1. Submit the provider application through the provider portal.
2. Upload current licenses, certifications, malpractice insurance, and CV.
3. The credentialing team reviews submitted documents.
4. Primary source verification is completed for all credentials.
5. The provider is presented to the credentialing committee for approval.

## Timeline
Standard credentialing takes 60 to 90 days from the date of a complete application.

## Re-credentialing
Providers must be re-credentialed every 3 years. The credentialing team will contact the
provider 90 days before expiration to begin the renewal process.

## Status Inquiries
Providers may check the status of their credentialing application through the provider
portal or contact the provider operations team.

## Important Note
This process is for operational onboarding only. It does not involve clinical care
decisions or patient-specific information.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2025.
